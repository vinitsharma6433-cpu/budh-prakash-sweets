Our Story

Forty-five years of the same measure

Shri Budh Prakash Sharma Budhu Halwai

In 1981, he opened a small counter in Ganj Mandi with one kadhai, one weighing scale and one rule: nothing leaves the shop that he would not serve at home. Across Sardhana, people still remember him fondly by that name — it became synonymous with honest, fresh mithai.

That rule has not changed. Today his legacy is carried forward by Shri Sanjay Kumar Sharma, who keeps the same measure, the same method and the same promise alive for the next generation. Khoya is still made fresh and finished the same day, in small batches. Families who bought their first box of barfi here now send their grandchildren for it.

Weddings, teej, Diwali, a simple Sunday craving — for Sardhana, it has been the same address for forty-five years.
